## Free tools we maintain

- **[WDF AI Metadata Remover](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wdf-ai-metadata-remover/)**: a free WordPress plugin that scans your media library and removes EXIF, XMP, IPTC and C2PA (AI) metadata without re-compressing your images.
- **[Image Metadata Remover](https://webdesignerfactory.com/tools/image-metadata-remover/)**: the same cleaning as a free online tool. It runs in your browser, so your images are never uploaded.
